Drip Mailer

Personalised outreach at scale, sent from your own Streamax mailbox. Build a signature, write once with merge fields, upload your list, test it on yourself, then send.

1 Mailbox

Mail is sent as you, so your mail password is needed to authenticate to the server. It is used for this send only — never stored, never logged, never written to the session cookie. It is cleared when you leave the page.

2 Signature

Rendered by the server, so what you see below is exactly what recipients get.

The prefilled image is a stock placeholder — replace it with your own photo before sending to customers.
Loading…

3 Compose

Merge fields: {first_name} {last_name} {company} {role} {email} — any column in your CSV works. A field with no value shows as [first_name] in the preview so gaps are obvious.

4 Recipients

CSV with columns Email, First_Name, Last_Name, Company, Role. Duplicates and invalid addresses are dropped and reported before anything sends.

Download the CSV template

5 Test, then send

Always send yourself a test first — it is the only way to see the signature, images and merge fields as a recipient will.
