# Competitor Financials & Corporate Status — verified September 2026

**Why this file exists.** Sales and leadership repeatedly asked Jerry hard
financial questions about competitors ("is Samsara actually profitable?",
"do they have a cash-flow problem?", "did Motive IPO?") and Jerry had only a
single stale ARR figure. Everything below is from public filings, earnings
releases and reputable trade press, each line dated. **Numbers here have a
shelf life — always state the as-of date when you quote them.**

---

## Samsara (NYSE: IOT) — the answer is "yes, they are profitable now"

The old talking point that Samsara is burning cash to buy market share is
**out of date**. Do not use it. It will be corrected in the room and it costs
credibility.

| Metric | Latest | As of |
|---|---|---|
| Ending ARR | **$1.991B** (+30% YoY) | Q1 FY2027, reported 4 Jun 2026 |
| Ending ARR | $1.9B (+30% YoY cc) | full FY2026, reported 5 Mar 2026 |
| Net new ARR, FY26 | $432M (+21% cc) | FY2026 |
| Quarterly revenue | $478.8M (+31% YoY) | Q1 FY2027 |
| GAAP EPS | **$0.08 — third consecutive profitable quarter** | Q1 FY2027 |
| Adjusted free cash flow | **$208.7M**, 13% FCF margin | full FY2026 |
| Non-GAAP operating margin | 16% full year (21% in Q4) | FY2026 |
| $100k+ ARR customers | 3,194 (+204 in the quarter) | Q4 FY2026 |
| Revenue from $100k+ customers | $1.2B (+37% YoY) | FY2026 |

**How to use this.** Samsara is no longer a "will they survive" story — they
are a scaled, GAAP-profitable competitor approaching $2B ARR. Attacking them
on viability is a losing line. The durable arguments are unchanged and are
**structural, not financial**:

- They sell **direct**, which is an existential threat to a TSP's own book.
  That argument gets *stronger*, not weaker, as Samsara gets healthier.
- Price: Streamax lands 25–37% below Samsara at the fleet level (see
  `07_competitor_pricing_2026.md`).
- Their model is NA-trained; EU/JP/LatAm driving conditions are a real
  product gap, not a marketing one.

⚠️ **Next print: Q2 FY2027 is due on or about 3 September 2026.** If today's
date is past that, this table is one quarter stale — say so.

## Motive (Keep Truckin) — filed, not yet public

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Status | **Still private.** S-1 filed 23 Dec 2025; NYSE ticker reserved as **MTVE**. Not yet priced as of Sep 2026. |
| ARR | $501M as of 30 Sep 2025, +27% YoY |
| Revenue | $327.3M for the 9 months to Sep 2025, +22% YoY |
| Net loss | $138.5M (9M FY25) — **unlike Samsara, still loss-making** |
| NDR | 126% on large customers |
| Gross margin | ~70% including hardware |
| Valuation | ~$3B (Series F, $7.80/share, Jul 2025); secondaries have traded **below $6**, i.e. a down-round signal |
| Backers | Alphabet's GV, Base10, Greenoaks, Index, Kleiner Perkins, Scale VP |

**How to use this.** Motive is the one where the financial-pressure argument
is still live: loss-making, IPO pending, secondary marks below the last
primary. An IPO-track company under margin scrutiny tends to (a) push price
discipline and (b) chase logo count. Both are exploitable. Do not claim they
are "failing" — they are growing 27%.

## Market structure (Berg Insight)

- Berg ranks **Streamax, Lytx and Samsara as leaders in their respective
  categories** — Streamax's leadership is in its own category, so quote this
  precisely rather than as "Berg says we're #1".
- North America is **~3× the size of** the European market; Europe is still
  **largely the UK**.
- NA + Europe installed base: **17 million units by 2029**.
- Top 7 (Verizon Connect, Geotab, Lytx, Netradyne, Samsara, SmartWitness,
  Trimble) ≈ **42% of the 2024 market** — i.e. the market is still more than
  half long-tail. That is the TSP/white-label opportunity in one number.
